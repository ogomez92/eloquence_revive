# The schwa and the a, moved apart

Castilian's five vowels and the three Catalan adds sit in one space, and two
of them were too near each other to be told apart:

        F1    F2              F1    F2
    i   280  2100         @   600  1500   as it was
    e   480  1800         E   600  1750
    a   660  1400         O   580  1000
    o   480   900
    u   300   750

Sixty hertz in the first formant and a hundred in the second, where no two of
IBM's own five are within two hundred of each other -- and casa has both
vowels in it, one after the other.

Four settings, each the pair rather than either alone: the schwa in towards
rest and the a further open.

    schwa        a
    600 1500   660 1400   A   as it was
    580 1520   680 1380   B   in the tree
    560 1550   700 1350   C
    540 1570   720 1330   D

Measured on the a and the schwa of casa, as the envelope's turning point in
each formant band:

          a            schwa
    A  625 1400     525 1450     50 Hz apart in f2
    C  650 1350     525 1575    225 Hz apart

B is what a listener picked. The schwa is `cat_ph_schwa_vals` in
`lang/caes/rules/ss_ssval.up` and the a is `stanspan_ph_a` in
`lang/caes/rules/ss_ssval.dr`, which is the only edit in that file that is not
IBM's.
